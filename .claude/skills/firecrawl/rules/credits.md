# Firecrawl credit policy

Approved by Valdemar on 3 November 2026. This supersedes the former ask-before-every-call credit rule.

- Ordinary search, scraping, mapping, documentation lookup and bounded research may use the existing account's credits automatically when they help the requested task.
- Check status before a paid task and choose a reasonable aggregate task budget. Ask before bulk work expected to exceed 100 credits in total. Do not split a task into smaller calls to avoid that threshold.
- Use page limits, PDF page caps and `--max-credits` where supported. If the total is unknown, start with a bounded sample that fits the remaining task budget, inspect actual usage and stop before an unapproved expansion beyond 100 credits. A crawl limit counts pages, not necessarily credits; PDF pages and extra extraction formats can cost more.
- Recurring monitors, newly paid data providers, credit purchases, paid subscriptions and plan upgrades require approval before creation, execution or acceptance. Discovery and reading an existing provider's contract do not authorize its paid execution. Respect provider terms; never accept them on the user's behalf without explicit approval.
- Never exceed available credits or enable overages automatically. Authentication or insufficient-credit errors are terminal for that call: check status once, then report the issue rather than retrying.
- Reuse current results and report observed usage when meaningful. Privacy, source restrictions, outreach approvals and other spending rules still apply. This policy alone does not authorize uploading private documents or sending private code, chat, secrets or client data to Firecrawl.
