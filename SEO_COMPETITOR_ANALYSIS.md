## 1. Competitor Overview

### 1.1 CALCA ApS
- **URL:** https://calca.dk
- **Positioning:** Specialist in DGNB certification and LCA calculations for building projects. Based in Storvorde (Aalborg area). Positions as a full-service sustainability consultancy for construction.
- **Services:** DGNB auditor/certification, LCA calculations (building projects + building process A4/A5), LCC calculations, daylight calculations, energy calculations, construction budgeting (kalkulationer), LCA training courses, SIGMA tool for LCA.
- **Target audience:** Entrepreneurs, architects, engineers.
- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed. Contact-based pricing.
- **Meta description:** "Med ekspertise indenfor LCA-beregninger, DGNB-certificeringer og faglig rådgivning hjælper vi med at navigere i de komplekse klimakrav."
- **Content strength:** Very strong topical coverage. Dedicated pages for each service. Good use of regulatory context (2025 climate requirements). Has reference projects page. Blog content about building process LCA (A4/A5 phases). Offers LCA training courses.
- **SEO notes:** Ranks for "LCA beregning" and "LCA rådgiver byggeri". Multiple service pages create good internal linking. Uses Elementor/WordPress. Content is well-structured with H1/H2/H3 hierarchy.

### 1.2 Energiberegner ApS
- **URL:** https://www.energiberegner.dk
- **Positioning:** Energy calculation specialists who also offer LCA. Positions as a one-stop shop for all BR18 documentation needs (energy, LCA, indoor climate, heat loss). Targets both private homeowners and professionals.
- **Services:** Energy frame calculations, LCA/climate calculations, heat loss calculations, LCA analysis, profitability calculations, indoor climate simulation (BSim), general consulting.
- **Target audience:** Both private (homeowners) and professional (architects, engineers). Dual-track website.
- **Pricing:** **Publicly listed.** Energy calculation from 5,000 DKK incl. moms. Energy + LCA bundle from 12,500 DKK incl. moms (typical single-story parcelhus). Delivery: 3-5 working days.
- **Meta description:** "Energiberegner tilbyder ekspertise i energiberegning, indeklima og LCA-beregninger. Vi hjælper både private og virksomheder."
- **Content strength:** Excellent blog content ("Hvad er en LCA-beregning" article ranks well). Strong educational content with table of contents, structured FAQ-style answers. Project type navigator helps users find the right service. Separate pages for 7 project categories per BR18.
- **SEO notes:** Very strong organic presence. Ranks for "LCA beregning" and related informational queries. Extensive internal linking through project types. Uses schema markup. Phone number prominently displayed (50 160 160).

### 1.3 DMR A/S
- **URL:** https://www.dmr.dk
- **Positioning:** Large environmental/engineering consultancy. LCA is one of many services. Positions as technically rigorous with proprietary tools (DMR Portal). Emphasizes transparency ("not black box or one-click").
- **Services:** LCA calculations (screening, design, documentation), LCC calculations, DGNB certification support, EPD creation for manufacturers, A4/A5 phase documentation, hotspot analysis, variant studies.
- **Target audience:** Entrepreneurs, architects, advisors, building owners, material suppliers.
- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed. Contact-based (named contact: Mikkel Volden, LCA team lead).
- **Meta description:** "DMR hjælper entreprenører, arkitekter, rådgivere, bygherrer og leverandører med LCA-sparring og fuldt outsourcede LCA-beregninger."
- **Content strength:** Good technical depth. Explains LCA lifecycle phases with graphics. Mentions proprietary database tool approved for DGNB. EPD services add credibility. Content covers the full project lifecycle (screening -> design -> documentation).
- **SEO notes:** Ranks for "LCA rådgiver byggeri". The page is nested deep in the site architecture (dmr.dk/baeredygtigt-byggeri/lca-lcc/). Massive overall domain authority from their broad environmental consulting practice.

### 1.4 NBS Nordic
- **URL:** https://www.nbsnordic.dk
- **Positioning:** Digital SaaS tool for LCA calculations, not a consultancy. Self-service platform where users create an account and perform their own LCA calculations. Integrated with BIM models.
- **Services:** Digital LCA tool (self-service), BIM/building specification sync, export to LCAbyg and Excel, ECO Platform EPD integration, generic and custom LCA data.
- **Target audience:** Advisors, engineers, architects who want to do their own LCA work.
- **Pricing:** Free account creation (no credit card required). Freemium/SaaS model.
- **Meta description:** "Med NBS Nordic får du et integreret LCA værktøj som du kan bruge til din LCA analyse. Vores LCA værktøj kan hjælpe dig med at lave LCA beregninger med få klik."
- **Content strength:** Good educational content explaining LCA. Video tutorials. Technical integration details (BIM sync, EPD data). Clear call-to-action for free signup.
- **SEO notes:** Ranks #3 for "LCA beregning Danmark". Strong title tag optimization ("LCA beregner -> Nem og gratis LCA beregning og analyse"). YouTube integration for engagement.

### 1.5 NIRAS
- **URL:** https://www.niras.dk
- **Positioning:** Major international engineering consultancy. LCA is a small part of their massive service portfolio. Authority play — positions on decades of experience and advanced tools (SimaPro).
- **Services:** LCA calculations from early phases, hotspot analysis, variant analysis, renovation scenarios, design optimization, energy production assessment.
- **Target audience:** Large building projects, public sector, major developers.
- **Pricing:** Not listed. Enterprise/project-based pricing. Named contacts with director-level titles.
- **Meta description:** "NIRAS har omfattende erfaring med livscyklusvurderinger (LCA). Analyserne bruger vi til optimering af byggeriet, design-, konstruktions- og materialevalg."
- **Content strength:** Concise, authoritative page. Strong breadcrumb structure. Named expert contacts with photos build trust.
- **SEO notes:** Ranks for "LCA rådgiver byggeri" and "livscyklusvurdering byggeri". Massive domain authority. Thin content on the specific LCA page (~4,100 chars) but domain strength compensates.

## 4. Content Gaps

### What Competitors Have That Din LCA Hjælper Lacks

1. **Educational blog content** — Energiberegner has extensive "what is LCA" articles that capture informational search intent. CALCA covers regulatory updates. Din LCA Hjælper has no blog.

2. **Project type segmentation** — Energiberegner breaks down services by building type (enfamiliehus, sommerhus, erhverv, tilbygning, ombygning). This captures long-tail searches and helps users self-qualify.

3. **Regulatory explainer content** — Multiple competitors explain BR18 requirements, 2025 changes, grensevaerdier (limit values), and who is responsible. This is critical for capturing "researcher" traffic.

4. **Reference projects / case studies** — CALCA has a dedicated reference projects page. NIRAS shows project imagery. Din LCA Hjælper has no visible portfolio.

5. **FAQ pages** — The structured data exists in Din LCA Hjælper's schema markup, but there is no visible FAQ section that search engines can use for featured snippets.

6. **Process explanation** — CALCA explains the LCA process through building phases (screening -> projektering -> aflevering). DMR shows the same flow. This builds trust and reduces uncertainty.

7. **Named team / credentials** — NIRAS shows named experts with photos and titles. DMR names their LCA team lead. CALCA shows contact info. Din LCA Hjælper's personal brand is underutilized.

8. **A4/A5 phase content** — The new July 2025 requirements for documenting transport and building process emissions. CALCA has a full page on this. This is directly relevant given Din LCA Hjælper's A45 platform.

9. **Glossary / terminology pages** — Energiberegner has an "ordbog" (glossary) with definitions for LCA terms. These capture long-tail informational queries.

## 6. Recommendations for Din LCA Hjælper

### Immediate (High Impact, Low Effort)

1. **Create a dedicated pricing page or section** — You are the cheapest option in the market at 3,500 DKK. This is your #1 competitive advantage. Make it unmissable. Target "LCA beregning pris" keyword explicitly.

2. **Add a visible FAQ section** — You already have FAQ schema markup in your HTML, but the questions should be visible on-page for users and featured snippet eligibility. Expand to 8-10 questions covering regulatory requirements, process, timeline, and A4/A5.

3. **Optimize for "LCA beregning" as primary keyword** — Ensure the H1, title tag, and first paragraph explicitly use "LCA-beregning" (with hyphen, as Danes typically write it). The current meta description is good but the body content should reinforce this keyword cluster.

4. **Add delivery time** — Energiberegner prominently states "3-5 arbejdsdage." If Din LCA Hjælper can match or beat this, state it clearly.

### Short-term (1-3 months)

5. **Create 3-5 educational blog posts** targeting informational queries:
   - "Hvad er en LCA-beregning? Alt du skal vide som arkitekt"
   - "Nye klimakrav 2025: Hvad betyder det for dit byggeri?"
   - "LCA-beregning for sommerhuse — udfordringer og løsninger"
   - "A4 og A5 faserne: Dokumentation af byggeprocessens CO2"
   - "Grænseværdier for CO2 i byggeri: Oversigt 2025-2029"

6. **Create project type pages** — Segment by enfamiliehus, rækkehus, sommerhus, erhvervsbyggeri, tilbygning. Each page should target the long-tail keyword "[type] + LCA beregning" and explain specific requirements and pricing for that type.

7. **Build a reference/case study section** — Even 3-5 anonymized case studies showing "X m² parcelhus, Y kr, Z dage" would build significant trust. Include before/after CO2 values if possible.

8. **Leverage the A45 platform differentiator** — No competitor mentions an automated tool for A4/A5 phase documentation. This is a unique selling point that deserves its own dedicated page optimized for "A4 A5 dokumentation byggeri" and "byggeproces CO2 dokumentation."

### Medium-term (3-6 months)

9. **Create competitor comparison pages** — "Din LCA Hjælper vs [Competitor]" pages targeting "LCA beregning alternativ" and comparison queries. Focus on pricing transparency and specialization.

10. **Build a glossary** — Create a "LCA ordbog" with definitions for: LCA, LCC, EPD, DGNB, GWP, CO2-ækvivalenter, grænseværdi, hotspotanalyse, etc. Each term = one indexable page.

11. **Consider a free LCA tool or calculator** — NBS Nordic captures traffic with a free tool. Even a simple "Tjek om dit projekt kræver LCA" calculator based on building size/type could generate leads.

12. **Target "LCA rådgiver" keyword** — This is a high-intent search term where NIRAS, Norconsult, and DMR rank. Position as "din personlige LCA rådgiver" to compete on a personal/accessible level vs. the large firms.

## 7. Competitive Positioning Summary

Din LCA Hjælper occupies a strong but underexploited position:

**Unique advantages:**
- Lowest visible price point in the market (3,500 DKK vs ~7,500+ DKK for competitors)
- Specialized focus on LCA only (vs. competitors who bundle with energy, DGNB, etc.)
- A45 platform for A4/A5 documentation (no competitor offers this)
- Personal service (solo consultant vs. faceless firms)
- Direct targeting of small architect firms (underserved by NIRAS/Norconsult/DMR)

**Main threats:**
- Energiberegner's content machine and transparent pricing creates strong organic presence
- CALCA's breadth of services (DGNB + LCA + training) builds deeper client relationships
- Large firms (NIRAS, DMR, Norconsult) dominate branded/authority searches
- NBS Nordic's free tool captures DIY-minded architects

**Strategic position:** Din LCA Hjælper should position as "the affordable LCA specialist for small-to-medium architect firms" — emphasizing price, speed, personal service, and the unique A45 platform. The content strategy should focus on capturing informational search traffic through educational content, while the service pages should emphasize transparent pricing and fast turnaround as primary differentiators.
