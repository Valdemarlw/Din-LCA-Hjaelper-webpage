# Din LCA Hjælper — Content Strategy

## Target Audience
Small architecture/advisor firms (1-5 people) who lack in-house LCA competence and need help navigating BR18 climate requirements for their building projects.

## Key Pain Points We Address
1. They don't know what needs to be documented or how
2. They're uncertain about the regulations (BR18 climate requirements)
3. They don't have time or expertise to do LCA calculations themselves
4. They need someone reliable who makes the process simple

## Positioning
- Specialiseret i LCA-beregning for byggeri
- Competitive pricing, transparent process
- Easy to work with, responsive, handles everything from data to report
- NOT mentioning: student status, specific calculation tools used

## Tone
Professional but approachable. Not stiff/corporate. Confident without overselling. The kind of tone where an architect thinks "this person knows what they're doing and won't waste my time."

## Site Structure

### Page 1: Forside (Homepage)
Everything an architect needs to evaluate and reach out — in one scroll.

#### Section 1: Hero
- **Headline:** Something about making LCA simple/handled for architects
- **Subheadline:** We handle the LCA calculation so you can focus on the project
- **CTA:** "Få et tilbud"
- **Trust indicator:** "Fra 3.500 kr" visible early

#### Section 2: The Problem / Why You Need This
- LCA-beregning er lovpligtig for nybyggeri (BR18)
- Regulations are complex and changing
- You need correct documentation for ibrugtagningstilladelse
- Getting it wrong means delays and problems at færdigmelding

#### Section 3: What We Do (Services)
- LCA-beregning fra tidlig fase til myndighedsklar rapport
- Hotspot-analyse — vi identificerer de mest CO₂-tunge bygningsdele
- Materialeoptimering — vi foreslår alternativer hvis grænseværdien er i fare
- Opdatering ved færdigmelding med faktiske mængder
- A4/A5 dokumentation tilgængelig (via A45 platformen — nævnes som en kapabilitet, ikke en separat prislinje)

#### Section 4: Process Overview
Simple 4-step visual:
1. **Send dit projekt** — Tegninger, mængder og projektinfo
2. **Modtag tilbud** — Vi vurderer omfanget og sender et tilbud
3. **Tidlig beregning** — LCA-beregning med hotspot-analyse og evt. materialeoptimering
4. **Myndighedsklar rapport** — Opdateret beregning og dokumentation klar til kommunen

#### Section 5: Pricing
- **LCA-beregning fra 3.500 kr — inkl. adgang til A45 platformen**
- Prisen afhænger af projektets størrelse, kompleksitet og antal konstruktionstyper
- Inkluderer: gennemgang af tegninger, tidlig beregning, hotspot-analyse, materialeforslag ved overskridelse, opdatering ved færdigmelding, myndighedsklar rapport
- **+ adgang til A45 platformen til dokumentation af A4 og A5** — fremhævet som bonus/merværdi
- "Kontakt os med dit projekt, så giver vi en pris"

#### Section 6: CTA
- "Klar til at få styr på dit projekts LCA?"
- "Send os dine tegninger og få et tilbud"
- Button: "Få et tilbud" → links to Kontakt page

## A4/A5 Strategy
- Position A45 access as a KEY PART of the value proposition, not a side mention
- Frame it as: "you get LCA calculation + access to A45 for A4/A5 documentation" — makes the price feel like a bargain
- Include it prominently in the pricing section: "inkl. adgang til A45 platformen til A4/A5 dokumentation"
- DO NOT list a separate price for A4/A5
- DO NOT link directly to A45 pricing page (avoid price comparison)
- The A45 inclusion costs nothing to deliver but significantly increases perceived value
- This also funnels architects onto the A45 platform, building that user base
