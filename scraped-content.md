### Hero
**Heading:** Til de professionelle

### Value Proposition
Vi er jeres eksterne LCA ressource, der leverer kapacitet, compliance og klare beslutningsgrundlag, når I mangler tid, mandskab eller specialviden.

### Target Audience
Arkitekter, ingeniører, total- og hovedentreprenører samt bygherrer/ejendomsudviklere.

### Collaboration Models
Three approaches offered:
- Project-by-project ad hoc work for quick LCA needs
- Framework agreements with fixed pricing and response times
- White label services operating under the client's name

### Core Deliverables
- Early LCA calculations following BR18 standards, including A4 (transport) and A5 (site/execution) estimations
- Hotspot analysis identifying highest CO₂-emission areas
- Alternative material recommendations if threshold breach risks emerge
- Final calculation adjustments for construction changes
- Municipal-ready reporting documentation

All services customizable to partner-specific needs.

### Process
Five stages from kickoff alignment through municipal-ready LCA delivery.
